Hi, I'm Meet 👋🏻

I craft interfaces where business goals and user needs align

With 10+ years in UI/UX design, I specialize in end-to-end flows for complex SaaS products across AI, Web3, FinTech, AdTech, MarTech, and Trading domains. I create user-centric interfaces that balance business strategy with seamless user experience.
